# Candidate Profile Example

Paste a concise version of your resume/profile into `Workflow Configuration.candidateProfile` before running the workflow.

```text
Your Name
Target Role: AI Automation Specialist
Location: Remote / Your preferred regions
Contact: your-email@example.com | LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/your-profile/

Professional Summary
Automation-focused professional with hands-on experience building workflow automations, AI-assisted operations, and low-code integrations. Strongest fit is for roles involving n8n, AI agents, workflow orchestration, process improvement, and business automation.

Core Skills
n8n, AI automation, workflow automation, low-code tools, prompt engineering, tool orchestration, API integrations, CRM automation, operations automation, support automation, process optimization

Professional Experience
Automation Specialist | Example Company
Built workflow automations that reduced repetitive manual work and improved operational response time.
Integrated AI tools into business processes across marketing, operations, customer support, and internal reporting.
Collaborated with non-technical teams to translate business requirements into reliable automations.

Role Focus
AI Automation Specialist, n8n Workflow Builder, Automation Engineer, Operations Automation Specialist
```

Keep private details out of public repositories. Use this example as structure, then paste your real profile only inside your private n8n workflow.
